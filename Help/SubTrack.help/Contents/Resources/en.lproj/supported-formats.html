See what an FFmpeg build supports

Click Supported Formats in Settings ▸ FFmpeg. The sheet lists Containers, Video Codecs, Audio Codecs, and Subtitle Codecs for the build you’re running, each group collapsible and each row naming the format’s identifier and its full name. Type in the Filter field to narrow every group at once.

Reading is not writing

Each row carries two marks, and they mean different things. Containers are marked Read / Write, codecs Decode / Encode — and reading is not writing. A build can decode a codec perfectly and be unable to produce a single frame of it. That’s normal: practically every build reads more than it writes.

That asymmetry is exactly what’s behind the warning that the current FFmpeg can’t encode a transcode target you’ve chosen. Filter for the codec, look at the second mark, and you’ll see it.
