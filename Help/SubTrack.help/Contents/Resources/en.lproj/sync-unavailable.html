If presets aren’t syncing

A notice appears beneath the preset list in Settings ▸ Presets when something is wrong. Nothing appears when syncing is working — success needs no announcement.

No iCloud account is signed in

Open System Settings, click your name at the top, and sign in. If you’re already signed in, check that iCloud Drive is turned on — SubTrack’s storage travels with it.

SubTrack works perfectly well signed out. Your presets are saved on this Mac; they just stay on this Mac.

iCloud is unavailable on this Mac

Something outside SubTrack is preventing iCloud access — typically a device management profile on a work or school machine, or Screen Time restrictions. Whoever administers the Mac can say whether that’s intended.

iCloud reported an error

Usually temporary: a network problem, or iCloud being briefly unavailable. Syncing resumes on its own once the connection recovers, and nothing is lost in the meantime — your presets are always stored locally as well.

If it persists across restarts and your network is fine, check Apple’s system status page for iCloud.
