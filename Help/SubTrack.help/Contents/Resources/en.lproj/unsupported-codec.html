If a codec can’t be encoded

Sometimes SubTrack warns that the current FFmpeg can’t encode a codec you’ve chosen as a target. Deciding to convert is one thing; having the encoder to do it is another.

The two reasons

  • You’re running the App Store version. It bundles an FFmpeg build without the software H.264 and H.265 encoders, because App Store rules don’t permit their licence. Choose a target the build does support, or use the downloadable version — see Choose between the two versions of SubTrack.
  • You pointed SubTrack at your own FFmpeg and that build was compiled without the encoder. Builds vary enormously in what they include. Switch back to Built-in in Settings ▸ FFmpeg, or install a build with the encoder you need — see Use an FFmpeg build of your own.

To see exactly what the FFmpeg you’re running can read and write, open Settings ▸ FFmpeg and click Supported Formats.
