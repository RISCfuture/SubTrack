Sync presets between your Macs

Presets follow you. Save one on your laptop and it appears on your desktop, through your own iCloud account, with nothing to set up and no account to create.

Syncing is automatic whenever you’re signed into iCloud. There is no switch for it in SubTrack, because there is nothing to configure — it uses the iCloud account macOS is already signed into.

What syncs, and what doesn’t

Presets sync. That’s the saved rule sets and their names.

Nothing else does. Your queues, the files in them, their output folders, and your FFmpeg settings all stay on the Mac they were made on. That’s deliberate: a queue refers to files on a particular disk, and a queue full of paths that don’t exist on the other machine would be worse than no queue at all.

Both editions of SubTrack share one iCloud container, so presets saved in the App Store version appear in the downloadable version and the other way round.

If they stop syncing

A notice appears beneath the preset list when something is wrong, and it names which of three things it is — see If presets aren’t syncing.

Your presets are private

They’re stored in your personal iCloud container. Nobody else can read them — not other users, and not SubTrack’s developer, who has no access to your iCloud data at all. See What SubTrack sends, and what it doesn’t.
